Used as the water colour lookup by Shaders/FallingHours.shader in the Falling
Hours waterfall room. The animated flow and geometry are original to this world.
The source project's Shader Graph is not imported: this world uses Unity's
built-in rendering pipeline and needs a separate inexpensive Quest shader.
Only this palette is reused; the original 4D Voronoi shader is not included.
